Privacy & cookies

What this website keeps, what it does not, and how to change your mind.

The short version. This site sets no advertising and no tracking cookies. It keeps two small items on your own device — the cookie choice you made and your accessibility settings — and nothing else. We do not sell anything about you.

Who we are

Caribbean Queen, 507 NW 5th Ave, Gainesville, FL 32601. Phone (352) 374-8111. We run this website and we are responsible for anything it stores on your device. This page covers this website only — the map and review sites we link to are run by other companies and have their own policies, listed further down.

What this site collects

Nothing you type. There is no account, no login, no contact form, no newsletter signup, no online ordering and no payment on this site. Every page is a plain page of text and pictures. If you want to order, call (352) 374-8111.

Our host, Vercel, keeps ordinary server logs for a short period so the site can be run and kept secure — the page requested, the time, a browser identifier and an IP address. We do not use those logs to build any picture of you, and we cannot tie them to a name.

Cookies and storage

Strictly speaking, this site sets no cookies at all. What it uses instead is your browser’s own local storage, which works the same way from your point of view and is covered by the same rules, so we are telling you about it plainly:

Everything this site stores on your device
NameWhat it is forHow long
cq.consent.v1 Remembers the choice you made in the cookie notice, so you are not asked on every page. Strictly necessary. 12 months, then we ask again
cq.a11y.v1 Remembers your accessibility settings — text size, contrast, plain type, stopped animation and so on. Strictly necessary. Until you reset them or clear your browser

Both stay on your device. Neither is sent to us or to anyone else, and neither contains a name, a phone number or an identifier that could be used to follow you anywhere.

Analytics and marketing

The cookie notice offers two optional categories, Analytics and Social & marketing. As of the date at the foot of this page, nothing in either category is loaded — there is no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel and no advertising tag on this site. The switches are there so that if we ever do add a visitor count or a social measurement tag, it will not run at all until you have allowed it. Saying no leaves the site working exactly as it does now.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy Control signal, or the older Do Not Track header, we treat it as a refusal of both optional categories. They stay switched off and cannot be turned on while that signal is set, and the notice says so.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page, or the button at the top of this one. You can also clear this site’s storage in your browser’s own settings, which wipes both items above; the notice will then appear again on your next visit.

Other companies we link to

When you follow one of these links, or when your browser fetches something from them, you are dealing with that company under its own policy. We have no access to what they collect.

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site are served by Google. Your browser fetches them from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which means Google sees the request and your IP address. Google sets no cookies for this and states it does not use the data for profiling.
  • Google Maps — only when you tap a directions, map or reviews link.
  • Yelp — only when you click through to our listing. We embed nothing from Yelp, so it cannot see you here unless you choose to go there.
  • Vercel — our host, as described above.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade information about you — and because there is nothing to sell, that is not a promise we have to work at.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or follow you to other websites.
  • We do not send marketing email. We have no mailing list.

Your rights

Whichever law covers you, start by calling us on (352) 374-8111. We will answer within 30 days. In practice the honest answer to “what do you hold about me?” is almost always “nothing”, and we will say so plainly rather than pretend otherwise.

  • Florida and the United States. You can ask what we hold, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it or delete it, and ask us not to sell or share it. We do not sell or share personal information, and we honour Global Privacy Control as an opt-out signal.
  • EU and UK visitors. Our lawful basis for the two necessary storage items is your legitimate interest in a site that works and remembers your accessibility settings; for anything optional it is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from Cookie settings. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

This site is a restaurant menu. It is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. There is nowhere on the site for anyone of any age to enter information.

Changes to this page

If we add anything that collects or stores more than what is listed above, we will update this page and the cookie notice before it goes live, and the notice will ask you again.
